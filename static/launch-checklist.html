Phase 1: Go Live (Day 1)

CRITICAL ▼
1. Get YouTube Data API Key
10 MIN HIGH IMPACT FREE
Fixes 77 placeholder videos automatically. Enables batch search for embeddable videos.
  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com — sign in with any Google account
  2. Click the project dropdown → "New Project" → name it HistoryNews-Videos → Create
  3. Left sidebar → "APIs & Services" → "Library"
  4. Search for YouTube Data API v3 → click it → click "Enable"
  5. Left sidebar → "APIs & Services" → "Credentials"
  6. Click "+ Create Credentials" → "API Key"
  7. Copy the key (looks like AIzaSyD-XXXX...)
  8. Click "Edit API key" → Restrict to YouTube Data API v3 only → Save
  9. Run: python scripts/find-videos.py --api-key YOUR_KEY
2. Deploy to Netlify
15 MIN CRITICAL FREE
Your 172 articles are sitting on localhost. Get them live. Netlify auto-deploys from GitHub.
  1. Go to app.netlify.com/signup — sign up with GitHub
  2. Click "Add new site" → "Import an existing project"
  3. Select GitHub → authorize → select historynews repo
  4. Build settings: 
    Build command: hugo
Publish directory: public
Environment variable: HUGO_VERSION = 0.153.2
  5. Click "Deploy site" — wait ~60 seconds
  6. Your site is now live at random-name.netlify.app
  7. Go to Site settings → Domain management to add your custom domain later
3. Buy a Domain Name
5 MIN HIGH $12/YEAR
A real domain gives your site credibility and SEO authority. Check availability on Namecheap or Cloudflare Registrar.
  1. Go to namecheap.com or Cloudflare Registrar (cheapest, at-cost pricing)
  2. Search for your desired domain (e.g., historynews.org, historynewsdaily.com)
  3. Purchase ($8-15/year for .com)
  4. In Netlify: Site settings → Domain management → Add custom domain
  5. Update your DNS records (Netlify provides instructions)
  6. Update baseURL in config.toml to your new domain
  7. Update all Facebook social media links from historynews.example.com
4. Add Privacy-Friendly Analytics
5 MIN HIGH FREE - $9/MO
You can't improve what you can't measure. See which articles get traffic, where visitors come from, and what they click.
  1. Choose: Plausible ($9/mo, privacy-first) or Umami (free self-hosted) or Google Analytics (free, less private)
  2. Sign up and get your tracking snippet
  3. Add the snippet to layouts/_default/baseof.html just before </head>
  4. Commit and push — Netlify auto-deploys
  5. Wait 24 hours, then check your dashboard for first data
5. Set Up Cloudflare CDN
10 MIN MEDIUM FREE
Faster load times globally, free SSL certificate, caching, and DDoS protection.
  1. Go to cloudflare.com — create free account
  2. Click "Add a Site" → enter your domain
  3. Select the Free plan
  4. Cloudflare scans your DNS records — review and confirm
  5. Change your domain's nameservers to Cloudflare's (instructions provided)
  6. Wait ~24 hours for propagation
  7. Enable: SSL/TLS → Full, Speed → Auto Minify, Caching → Browser Cache TTL: 1 month

Skip this step if you used Cloudflare Registrar for your domain — it's already configured.

Phase 2: Get Found (Day 2)

SEO ▼
6. Submit to Google Search Console
5 MIN CRITICAL FREE
Google won't index your 172 articles unless you tell it they exist. Submit your sitemap.
  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console
  2. Click "Add property" → enter your domain
  3. Verify ownership (DNS TXT record or HTML file — Cloudflare makes DNS easy)
  4. Go to "Sitemaps" in the left sidebar
  5. Enter: sitemap.xml → click "Submit"
  6. Google will begin crawling your 172 articles within 24-48 hours
7. Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools
5 MIN MEDIUM FREE
Bing powers DuckDuckGo and Yahoo search too. Same process as Google.
  1. Go to bing.com/webmasters
  2. Sign in with Microsoft account
  3. Click "Import from Google Search Console" (fastest) or add manually
  4. Submit your sitemap: sitemap.xml
8. Build Era Taxonomy Pages
30 MIN HIGH FREE
Fix the Hugo taxonomy warning and create SEO-friendly landing pages like /eras/world-war-ii/ that rank in Google.
  1. Create layouts/_default/taxonomy.html and layouts/_default/term.html
  2. The taxonomy page lists all eras; the term page shows articles in that era
  3. Add eras = "eras" to the [taxonomies] section in config.toml (already done)
  4. Run hugo — the warning should disappear
  5. Each era now has its own URL: /eras/world-war-ii/, /eras/cold-war/, etc.
9. Add Breadcrumb Navigation
15 MIN MEDIUM FREE
Breadcrumbs (Home > Articles > Era > Title) appear in Google search results and improve click-through rate.
  1. Create layouts/partials/breadcrumbs.html with structured data
  2. Add BreadcrumbList JSON-LD schema
  3. Include the partial at the top of layouts/articles/single.html
  4. Style with CSS for visual breadcrumbs
  5. Test with Google Rich Results Test
10. Create a Human-Readable Sitemap Page
15 MIN MEDIUM FREE
An A-Z listing of all 172 articles on one page. Google loves crawling these, and visitors use them for discovery.
  1. Create content/sitemap-page.md with a custom layout
  2. The template loops through all articles and displays them grouped by era
  3. Link it from the footer and nav menu

Phase 3: Automate Content (Day 3)

AUTOMATION ▼
11. Build "This Day in History" Page
2 HRS VERY HIGH FREE
Fresh content every single day using the free Wikipedia "On This Day" API. Zero manual work. The #1 traffic driver for history sites.
  1. Create content/today-in-history.md with a custom layout
  2. The page loads today's events via JavaScript from:
    https://api.wikimedia.org/feed/v1/wikipedia/en/onthisday/all/MM/DD
  3. Display events, births, and deaths for today's date
  4. Cross-reference against your articles — highlight matches with "Read our full article" links
  5. Add to nav menu: Home | Articles | Today | About
12. Schedule Daily Social Media Posts
30 MIN HIGH FREE TIER
You already have 150 social posts in CSV format. Upload them to Buffer or Hootsuite and schedule for the next 6 months.
  1. Sign up for Buffer (free: 3 channels, 10 posts each)
  2. Connect your Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok accounts
  3. Open social-media/social-posts-batch1.csv and batch2.csv
  4. Use Buffer's Bulk Upload feature to import the CSV
  5. Set a posting schedule (e.g., 9 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM)
  6. For "This Day in History" daily posts, create a recurring template
13. Set Up GitHub Actions CI/CD
30 MIN HIGH FREE
Auto-run quality-check.py and video embed check on every push. Rejects bad content before it goes live.
  1. Create .github/workflows/quality-check.yml in your repo
  2. The workflow should: 
    on: [push, pull_request]
jobs:
  quality-check:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      - uses: actions/setup-python@v5
      - run: python scripts/quality-check.py
      - run: python scripts/check-video-embeds.py
  3. Push the file — GitHub Actions runs automatically
  4. You'll see a green ✓ or red ✗ on every commit
14. Create Article Template Snippet
10 MIN MEDIUM FREE
Type newhistory + Tab in VS Code and get a full article scaffold with all fields pre-filled.
  1. In VS Code: File → Preferences → Configure User Snippets → markdown
  2. Add a snippet with all front matter fields pre-filled
  3. Use tab stops ($1, $2, etc.) for fields you need to fill in
  4. Alternatively, use the existing python scripts/new-article.py CLI tool
15. Set Up RSS-to-Email Pipeline
20 MIN HIGH FREE TIER
Hugo generates RSS. Connect to Buttondown or Mailchimp — every new article auto-sends to subscribers.
  1. Sign up for Buttondown (free up to 100 subscribers) or Mailchimp (free up to 500)
  2. In settings, find "RSS to Email" or "RSS Campaign"
  3. Enter your RSS feed URL: https://yourdomain.com/index.xml
  4. Set frequency: weekly digest recommended
  5. Add a signup form to your site (embed code in footer or sidebar)

Phase 4: Build Audience (Day 4-5)

GROWTH ▼

30 MIN VERY HIGH FREE
"Get our free PDF: 10 History Moments That Changed Everything" in exchange for email. This is how you build a repeat audience.
  1. Create a 1-page PDF from your 10 best article summaries (Canva or Google Docs)
  2. Host the PDF on your site: static/downloads/top-10-history-moments.pdf
  3. Set up a signup form in Buttondown/ConvertKit with auto-delivery of the PDF
  4. Add a signup box to: article pages (after the body), homepage sidebar, and footer

17. Create a "Start Here" Page
20 MIN MEDIUM FREE
Curated list of your 10 best articles for new visitors. Reduces bounce rate and helps people fall in love with your site.
  1. Create content/start-here.md
  2. Pick your 10 most engaging articles (mix of positive + dramatic)
  3. Write a brief intro: "New here? Start with these 10 stories."
  4. Add to nav menu between Home and Articles
18. Add History Quizzes
1 HR HIGH FREE
JavaScript quiz at the bottom of articles ("Test what you learned!"). Increases time on site and makes results shareable.
  1. Create a quiz partial: layouts/partials/quiz.html
  2. Store quiz questions in article front matter as a YAML list
  3. Build a simple JS quiz engine (multiple choice, 5 questions)
  4. Show score at the end with a "Share your score!" button
  5. Start with 10 articles, then expand
19. Set Up a Newsletter
15 MIN HIGH FREE
Weekly "This Week in History" email. Repurpose your existing articles — no new writing needed.
  1. Sign up for Substack (free, built-in audience) or Ghost (free self-hosted)
  2. Create a weekly template: 3 article summaries + links back to your site
  3. Schedule for Sunday mornings (best open rates for history content)
  4. Cross-promote: link to newsletter signup from every article
20. Create Pinterest Pins
1 HR HIGH FREE
History is HUGE on Pinterest. Each article image + headline = a pin. Use Canva's bulk create from CSV.
  1. Create a Pinterest Business account
  2. Design a pin template in Canva (1000×1500px)

Phase 5: Revenue & Sustainability

MONETIZE ▼
21. Apply for Google AdSense
15 MIN MEDIUM FREE
Passive income from ad placements. History sites get $5-15 RPM. Wait until you have consistent traffic (~1,000 visits/month).
  1. Wait until your site has 30+ days of consistent content and traffic
  2. Go to google.com/adsense — apply
  3. Add the verification snippet to your baseof.html
  4. Wait 1-14 days for approval
  5. Place ad units: one below the article header, one between body sections
  6. Don't overdo it — 2-3 ads per article maximum
22. Add Book Affiliate Links
1 HR MEDIUM FREE
Your articles cite books in the sources section. Replace plain text with affiliate links to Amazon or Bookshop.org.
  1. Sign up for Amazon Associates or Bookshop.org Affiliates
  2. For each book cited in your articles, get the affiliate link
  3. Update the sources section to link to the book with your affiliate tag
  4. Bookshop.org gives 10% commission and supports independent bookstores
  5. Disclose: add "Some links are affiliate links" to your About page

Phase 6: Future-Proofing

MAINTENANCE ▼
24. Set Up Automated Link Checking
15 MIN MEDIUM FREE
GitHub Actions runs a weekly link check that catches dead source URLs, broken images, and removed YouTube videos.
  1. Create .github/workflows/weekly-link-check.yml
  2. Schedule with cron: schedule: [{cron: '0 8 * * 1'}] (every Monday 8 AM)
  3. Use lycheeverse/lychee-action@v1 to check all links in markdown files
  4. Run python scripts/check-video-embeds.py to verify videos
  5. Send results to email or Slack on failure
25. Build a Content Calendar with AI
30 MIN HIGH FREE
Script that checks which "This Day in History" dates match articles you DON'T have yet, and suggests new topics ranked by search volume.
  1. Build a script that maps all your article historydate fields to calendar dates
  2. Cross-reference with Wikipedia's "On This Day" data for every day of the year
  3. Identify gaps: dates with major events but no matching article
  4. Use Google Trends API or SEMrush (free tier) to rank gaps by search interest
  5. Generate a priority list: "Write about [EVENT] — 50K monthly searches, no article yet"
  6. Schedule 1-2 new articles per week targeting the highest-traffic gaps
