About History News

Who We Are

History News is a digital publication dedicated to telling the world’s most dramatic historical stories the way they deserve to be told — with primary sources, rare photographs, and writing that puts you right in the middle of the action.

We believe history isn’t dusty and boring. It’s full of heroes and villains, impossible odds, terrible mistakes, and moments that changed everything. Our job is to bring those moments to life.

Our Mission

We have one simple mission: make history feel like breaking news.

Every article on this site is built around original sources — the actual documents, photographs, newspaper pages, and recordings from the events we cover. We don’t just tell you what happened. We show you the evidence, point you to the archives, and let you explore the primary sources yourself.

How We Work

Every History News article follows the same standards:

  • Primary sources first. We start with the original documents, photographs, and recordings — not with other people’s interpretations
  • Verified facts. Every claim is cross-referenced against multiple reliable sources
  • Clear writing. We write at a level that’s accessible to everyone — you shouldn’t need a history degree to understand history
  • Full citations. Every article includes a sources section so you can verify our work and explore further
  • Free-use images. We prioritize images from public domain collections like the Library of Congress, Smithsonian Open Access, National Archives, and Wikimedia Commons

Our Sources

We draw from the world’s greatest archives and collections:

  • Library of Congress — America’s largest library, with millions of digitized photographs, maps, and documents
  • National Archives (NARA) — The official repository of U.S. government records
  • British National Archives — The UK’s equivalent, holding centuries of government records
  • Smithsonian Institution — America’s museum system, with millions of artifacts and images released under open access
  • Europeana — Europe’s digital cultural heritage platform
  • Internet Archive — A digital library preserving websites, books, and media
  • Chronicling America — The Library of Congress’s free database of historic American newspapers
  • JSTOR — Academic journals and primary source collections
  • Project Gutenberg — Free digital copies of public domain books
  • CIA FOIA Reading Room — Declassified intelligence documents
  • And many more

Contact

History News is an independent publication. We welcome corrections, suggestions, and story ideas.

A Note on Images

The photographs and illustrations used on this site are sourced from public domain archives whenever possible. We make every effort to provide accurate attribution and credit. If you believe an image has been incorrectly attributed, please let us know.
